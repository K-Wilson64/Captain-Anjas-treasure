Who is Captain Anja?

Captain Anja was a pirate witch who called a secret island hidden in purple mist her home. Then one night, she and her ship vanished, leaving behind a mysterious map and a warning: “Some treasures are hidden for a reason.” Rumor says she concealed her most powerful spells across the island, waiting for someone clever enough to uncover them. Did she leave them behind to be found? or was she trying to keep something locked away?
